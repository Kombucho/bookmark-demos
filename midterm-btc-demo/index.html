What BTC did after three midterms

The last three US midterms, drawn as straight paths and scaled from a price you type. Each line falls to the low the post names, over the number of days it names, then rises or settles at the percent a year after the election.

Implied by the post’s scaled lows, roughly $78,000. Not a live quote.

Three data points are not a forecast. The post describes three past midterms. It does not say the next one will do this.

Scaled paths

Straight segments between the stated points. Not a daily price series. The year-after point is drawn at day 365, this page’s stand-in for “a year.”

Hover the chart, or focus it and use the arrow keys. The readout is the schematic price on that day.

Source

From @dealerdefi’s post: “here’s what btc did after the last 3 [US midterms], scaled to today’s price: 2014: −48% in 71 days → ~$40.5K; 2018: −51% in 39 days → ~$38.4K; 2022: −15% in 13 days → ~$66.3K; each of those lows was the bear market bottom; a year after the election: +24% / +45% / +92%.”

The lines are Mermaid xychart-beta, drawn by Pele 1.7.0 (MIT), vendored as vendor/pele.global.js. The license is vendor/PELE-LICENSE.txt. The hover readout and the annotation boxes are this page. No price feed is called.
