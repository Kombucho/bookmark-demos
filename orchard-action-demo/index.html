One spend, one output

An Orchard action always spends one note and creates one note. That fixed shape is how a dummy hides. This page is a picture of that, on a depth-4 tree. It does not hold keys, funds, or a proof.

Every hash here is SHA-256 from WebCrypto, labelled as a stand-in for Sinsemilla or Poseidon. The Orchard crate is not on this page. This page is schematic and not consensus crypto.
