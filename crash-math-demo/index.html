How deep is the hole

A loss of x leaves 1 − x. Getting back takes a gain of x / (1 − x), and at a yearly return r that is ln(1 / (1 − x)) / ln(1 + r) years. Below, the same idea on a seeded path: four layers you can switch, and the drawdown they leave behind.

From Every free resource on how elite funds survive a crash at hft.md/crash. Gain needed is x / (1 − x). Years to recover is ln(1 / (1 − x)) / ln(1 + r). Volatility targeting is σ target / σ now, from an EWMA of daily squared returns with λ = 0.94. The brake cuts exposure once the protected book is X% under its own peak. The trend layer holds less while price is under its moving average. The Kelly cap is a fraction of μ / σ², estimated from a trailing window of daily returns, and it never goes below zero.

The path is geometric Brownian motion with an injected crash multiplied through the crash window, so the injected factor compounds to the depth on the slider. The same seed draws the same path. A layer that steps aside after a fall can also miss the rebound. These figures are simulated educational data. They are not advice.
