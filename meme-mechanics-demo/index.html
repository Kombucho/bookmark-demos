Meme mechanics

KEEL is a fictional token on a desk that exists only in this page. The curve is constant-product. A per-wallet cap refuses an oversized order whole. Stakers share the trade fee. The treasury buys and burns when it fills. A transfer locks on Solana and mints on NEAR.

Nothing is signed or sent. OmniBridge and Chain Signatures are not contacted. The NEAR amounts are not a balance you can move.

Per-wallet cap 4,000 KEEL, counting the curve balance, the stake, and the minted balance. An order past the cap is refused whole. The fee is 3.00% of the NEAR side and does not enter the curve, so k stays the product of the reserves.

    Treasury rule
