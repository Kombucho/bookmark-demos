Checked rules are broken on the left and followed on the right. Uncheck one and both trays follow it, so the frame time is down to the rules you left on. “Expensive only” and “Rule-following only” freeze the other tray, which makes the number easier to blame.

From Emil Kowalski’s post on the animation-performance skill. Animated blur() stays under 20px, and 2–5px is enough to soften a crossfade. box-shadow repaints; the skill’s swap is filter: drop-shadow(). This bench fades a shadow that was painted once, which is opacity. An inherited custom property is not rewritten every frame. will-change is not set: the skill adds it only after a one-pixel shift. Nothing here uses transition: all.
