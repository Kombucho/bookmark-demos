    After Anna Lucia’s Unravel, a series of 128 moving tapestries on Art Blocks. The threads stay in motion and do not loop. As a token passes from wallet to wallet it loses its mint condition and frays into a new form, rather than dissolving. This page is a sketch of that idea. The palettes, the hidden wear thresholds, and the addresses are this folder’s, not the contract’s.

    Mint, lightly worn, frayed, and unravelling are labels on the wear so far. How much one transfer adds depends on the token and on which threshold the piece has already crossed. The scrubber only previews that wear. Reset returns the token to the address that minted it.
