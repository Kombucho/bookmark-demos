This page is a simulation of the Uzzy concept: one menu bar panel with what you’ve used, what’s left, and when each limit resets. The figures are made up and stay in this browser under bookmark-demos.usage-reset.v1. It does not read a Claude, Codex, Cursor, or Antigravity session, and it does not call a provider. From @akacronos’s post. Uzzy is free and open source for macOS: uzzy.app, aka-cronos/uzzy.
