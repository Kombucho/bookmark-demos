Quick between, slow inside

Auction market theory, as a path you can replay. Inside a balance zone the price rotates. Between zones it runs. The profile counts time at each price: the point of control, the 70% value area, and the thin gap. These numbers are this made-up path.

Synthetic and educational. This is not bitcoin, not a price history, and not trading advice. Nothing is loaded from the network.

Reduced motion is on. The path stays put until you press play or step.

Source

From @bull_genius’s post: “auction market theory teaches you that price moves QUICK between two equilibrium zones (market inefficiency zone/expansion) and SLOW inside them (market balance zone/contraction) the volume profile shows you the two balance zones market was auctioning in. it also shows you the gap where prices moved inefficiently.”

The post goes on to read a bitcoin chart. This page does not use that chart, those prices, or that liquidation figure. The generator is seeded and deterministic. Time at a price stands in for volume. The value area is 70% of the time in a balance zone, grown from the busiest price. A tie takes both neighboring prices. The lower price wins when two prices are equally busy.

The price line and the time-at-price bars are Mermaid xychart-beta, drawn by Pele 1.7.0 (MIT), vendored as vendor/pele.global.js. The license is vendor/PELE-LICENSE.txt. The bars are turned sideways so they share an upward price scale with the path. The bands and the annotation boxes are this page.
