Painted world

Hills, trees, and rocks from a seed. Walk through them. A paint pass turns the picture into strokes. The badge names the backend this browser actually used.

Brush 2

WASD or arrows walk. Drag the picture to look. On a phone, the left stick walks and the right stick looks.

The seed is the URL hash, so a copied link opens the same hills. The paint pass is a Kuwahara filter: each pixel keeps the steadiest nearby color, which reads as a stroke. Raw skips that pass.

From @dhof’s post on a wasm and WebGPU painted world, bash.tv/dhof/painted-world. This page is a short walk in a landscape generated here, with its own paint pass. It does not load that world, and it does not load Three.js.
