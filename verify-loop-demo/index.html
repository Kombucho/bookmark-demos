Deterministic simulation

Verify loop

A toy ledger compiles, in the only sense this page has, and its happy-path tests pass. The seed budget then reorders messages, drops an ack, crashes between a debit and a credit, and jumps a clock. The first broken invariant is the finding. The same seed replays it. A scripted fix goes in, and the budget runs again.

No model is called. This ledger is not Overalls, and this loop is not Antithesis. The faults and the four fixes are scripted so the discovery and the rectification stay on the page.

Happy path

    Seed budget

    Eight seeds. Each seed picks who pays whom and how far the replica clock jumps. The fault script is the same every time: deliver one transfer, reorder the next pair, drop an ack and retry, crash between the two legs, then read while the replica clock is ahead and its log is behind.
