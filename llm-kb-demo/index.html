LLM knowledge base

Raw notes stay as you wrote them. Compiling them builds a wiki: a page per note, a hub per company, backlinks, and an index. Ask a question and the answer names the pages it opened. A new note rewrites the pages it touches and leaves the rest.

Larkmill Ceramics, Brineharbor Works, and Vellum & Wick are fictional. The numbers were written for this page. No model is called, and nothing is fetched.

Source rank. An own note outranks a filing, a filing outranks a call, and a call outranks news. The hub keeps the higher source. The loser stays on the page as challenged, and on the article that quoted it.
