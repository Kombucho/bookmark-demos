# Bookmark demos

Fifty-eight static tools:

- [Spring UI](spring-ui-demo/) — a lamp toggle whose knob squashes and stretches, a button that compresses and overshoots, a note card that snaps home, a sheet that springs open, and a tab mark that slides past its word. Stiffness, damping, and mass are sliders. Stiff, just right, wobbly, and a straight line play side by side, and a plot shows the distance to the target. From [@yui540’s post](https://x.com/yui540/status/2120825584947487170), 「個人的に程良く弾力があるUIが好き。」 The springs are stepped in the page. Nothing is loaded from the network.
- [Micro-branches](micro-branch-demo/) — a phone card driven by design tokens. Fork a variant from any iteration, compare two side by side, and reject one with a one-line reason so a later fork skips that screen. A config panel tunes corner radius, spacing, accent, type size, and shadow, then bakes the values into a new branch and closes. The tree stays in localStorage. From [Vincent Nallatamby’s thread](https://x.com/VNallatamby/status/2118811242592907427) on Quest’s AI iOS designer workflow. No model is called.
- [After three midterms](midterm-btc-demo/) — type a starting bitcoin price (the default, roughly $78,000, is implied by the post, not a live quote) and see the 2014, 2018, and 2022 midterm paths: the stated drawdown over the stated days, then the one-year percent, scaled from that price. Hide a year and hover a day. The lines are schematic. From [@dealerdefi’s post](https://x.com/dealerdefi/status/2117163554105168082). Drawn with vendored [Pele](https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele) 1.7.0. Three points are not a forecast.
- [Sprite variants](sprite-variants-demo/) — pick a National Dex number and read the file path for front, back, shiny, and female, across the default PNGs, Dream World SVGs, official artwork, and Showdown GIFs. A missing file stays missing. Pixel frames stay crisp. From [@dextune’s post](https://x.com/dextune/status/2119097545620410461) on the [PokeAPI sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). The images are loaded from that repo’s raw files.
- [Local image lab](local-image-lab-demo/) — resize, then compress to JPEG or WebP and compare byte sizes. Sample a palette of HEX codes. Re-encode and parse the bytes to show the EXIF APP1 segment is gone. A sample picture is drawn on the page, so it runs with no file. From [@SubhanHQ’s post](https://x.com/SubhanHQ) sharing [Aimg](https://animg.vercel.app). Nothing is uploaded.
- [Straight lines](straight-line-demo/) — a portrait, a mountain range, and a spiral shaded only with straight strokes. Angle, spacing, and weight redraw the plate. Drag to close a region and hatch it. From [@pmamtraveller’s post](https://x.com/pmamtraveller/status/2118294851431092442) on Sunny Art’s straight-line drawings.
- [Text morph](text-morph-demo/) — a currency figure, a status line, and three metrics. Digits roll by place, and a letter both phrases share slides. Snap cuts. From [@GabiDev98’s bookmark](https://x.com/GabiDev98/status/2118235307216601291) of [Torph](https://torph.lochie.me) by lochieaxon. This page does not install the package.
- [Auction anything](auction-demo/) — a multi-bidder proxy auction using the rules from [visualizevalue/auctionanything](https://github.com/visualizevalue/auctionanything): private maxima, 10% steps, earlier ties, a 48-hour close, and a 10-minute anti-snipe.
- [Requirements review](requirements-demo/) — paste a note and see which of the 18 characteristics from [make-requirements-great](https://github.com/gnurio/nurijanian-skills/blob/main/skills/make-requirements-great/SKILL.md) fail, including verb + object duplicates.
- [Named embeddings](jev-embed-demo/) — edit questions, document scores, and the query vector from [Kieran Klaassen’s note](https://x.com/kieranklaassen/status/2103522599414501550). Rank is cosine similarity. No model is called.
- [Cinematic FUI](fui-demo/) — scroll a three.js HUD. Instanced quads blend into one black-and-white mask, sit on a tilted plane, and a final shader maps that mask through a palette with grain, blur, and chromatic aberration. From [kc’s post](https://x.com/bzor/status/2106094467992224005).
- [Agent change requests](change-requests-demo/) — review scripted edits to typed records. Writes land as change requests with field diffs, comments, and commits, including two requests that conflict on one field. From [Dan Kornas’s post](https://x.com/DanKornas/status/2105783312350421009) about [Busabase](https://github.com/busabase/busabase). No model is called.
- [A Lisp image](lisp-grow-demo/) — a tiny Lisp interpreter and a live picture. Step through a scripted conversation, or type a form. Redefining a function changes the running image; `*world*` survives. From [Geoffrey Huntley’s post](https://x.com/GeoffreyHuntley/status/2107080798209794317) and [article](https://ghuntley.com/lisp/). No model is called.
- [Theme studio](theme-studio-demo/) — hue, contrast, roundness, density, type, and shadow write CSS variables. A component gallery restyles live, and the page exports tokens plus a short design brief. From [Josh Puckett’s post](https://x.com/joshpuckett/status/2107175379903594714) about [Graphical](https://graphicalui.com).
- [Constraint gate](constraint-gate-demo/) — an agent and a hurried human send twelve diffs. Toggle the type checker, lint rules, tests, a locked abstraction, and alerts. Each rule stops a diff, or it ships and an incident follows. From [Lauren Tan’s post](https://x.com/poteto/status/2106916667599278365). No model is called.
- [Concept first](concept-first-demo/) — pick a running app, a sleep app, a plant app, or an Italian deck. Leave the template, choose one of three directions, and run a critique that counts the tells. Refine changes the phone screen. From [Tobia Donadon’s article](https://x.com/tobiadonadon_/status/2106430529113301163). No model is called.
- [Athanor](athanor-demo/) — planetary hours from sunrise and sunset, a sigil and a three-card spread from a host-key stand-in, four pigment stages with measured contrast, a Floyd-Steinberg plate, and a roguelike status line. From [Panat’s post](https://x.com/ptaranat/status/2106851920371741074) about [athanor](https://github.com/script-wizards/athanor).
- [Paper Mono](paper-mono-demo/) — a type bench for [Paper Mono](https://github.com/paper-design/paper-mono) v1.000 (SIL OFL 1.1): variable weight, coding ligatures, duospace, a narrow space, small arrows, a single-story a, a slashed zero, and fractions. From [Paper’s post](https://x.com/paper/status/2107867860349735284).
- [ASCII stage](ascii-stage-demo/) — a gallery and a live embed editor for [ascii.rest](https://ascii.rest) (MIT, by @bas3line). Pick a piece, edit the tag, and hold the first frame the way the library does for reduced motion. From [@inlovewithgo’s post](https://x.com/inlovewithgo/status/2107800389395636271).
- [Brand bot](brand-bot-demo/) — sample mentions of Mothglass, a fictional lantern, stream into four lanes. A rule book you can edit sorts them into amplify, product feedback, needs attention, and ignore, and names the words that matched. From [@ishverduzco’s post](https://x.com/ishverduzco/status/2107998499891728837). No model is called. No live account is read.
- [Animation cost](animation-cost-demo/) — run the same toast tray twice, with a large blur, an animated box-shadow, and a CSS variable rewritten every frame beside a small blur, a shadow layer faded with opacity, and a transform, and watch a requestAnimationFrame frame-time readout. From [Emil Kowalski’s post](https://x.com/emilkowalski/status/2109277388437868623) on the [animation-performance skill](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/main/skills/animation-performance/SKILL.md).
- [Solana accounts](solana-accounts-demo/) — inspect a simulated ledger of accounts, send a toy transaction, then turn signer, owner, type, overflow, and program-id checks off and run the attacks from the pitfalls list. From [Helius on the account model](https://x.com/Helius/status/1637656477510012928) and [the programming model](https://x.com/Helius/status/1724085702013395183), and from [Neodyme’s common pitfalls](https://blog.neodyme.io/posts/solana_common_pitfalls). No cluster is contacted.
- [Secret files](folder-tabs-demo/) — six hanging folders on a cabinet rail. Choose a tab and that jacket comes forward with a title, a date stamp, and a few lines of notes. From [@samdape’s hanging folders](https://x.com/samdape/status/1846447101779018087), via the [Kombai Selects post](https://x.com/kombaiselects/status/2103227978180079804).
- [Sharded query](sharded-query-demo/) — one join crosses a router and four Postgres shards. Step from auth to the rows coming back, then shard orders by customer so each shard joins locally. From [PlanetScale’s account of a sharded join](https://planetscale.com/blog/the-lifecycle-of-a-sharded-postgres-query), via @samlambert. No database is contacted.
- [Pair token](pair-token-demo/) — mint and redeem a share of a full-range ETH/NVDA pool, run swap flow, and compound trading fees with a 10% cut. The chart is one Pair Token against a 50/50 hold and an uncompounded LP. From [@OxSimpleFarmer’s post](https://x.com/OxSimpleFarmer/status/2110063423799345571). Nothing onchain is contacted.
- [Atomic compose](atomic-compose-demo/) — flash-borrow, swap on two venues, and repay. One L1 lands or reverts the whole route. With one venue on an L2, the legs settle apart and a failed leg leaves a position. From [toly’s post](https://x.com/toly/status/1743132883697410186). Nothing onchain is contacted.
- [Flash motion](flash-motion-demo/) — press Record and drag a shape. The timeline stores the path as offsets from the base pose, so moving the shape later replays the same performance. Trim a clip from either end. From [tldraw’s post](https://x.com/tldraw/status/2098108244686192722) and [Introducing tldraw flash](https://tldraw.dev/blog/introducing-tldraw-flash). No SDK, and no video export.
- [Upgrade authority](upgrade-authority-demo/) — four fictional vaults, each with a TVL and an upgrade authority: a single key, an N-of-M multisig, a governance council, or locked. Propose a drain and see which authorities let it land. A timelock queues a signature that already passed. From [Neodyme on upgrade authorities](https://neodyme.io/de/blog/solana_upgrade_authority/) and [the bookmarked thread](https://x.com/Neodyme/status/1591496755258040327). No cluster is contacted.
- [Design & engineering ops](design-ops-demo/) — Person A pulls a branch from Gospel and submits an exploration. Person B sends it back, or it merges into Design Gospel. Engineering pulls from its own Gospel, and the approved gospel becomes the app. From [Greg Washington’s diagram](https://x.com/gregmwashington/status/2110405679982723243), quoting [the operating model](https://x.com/gregmwashington/status/2107130016966623295). No model is called.
- [Anchor gotchas](anchor-gotchas-demo/) — colliding PDA seeds, a cash-out through `ctx.remaining_accounts`, a CPI signed with a pool PDA, and a mint supply that stays stale until `reload()`. Each case starts vulnerable. From [Zellic](https://www.zellic.io/blog/the-vulnerabilities-youll-write-with-anchor), via [the bookmarked post](https://x.com/zellic_io/status/1559982414608248832). No cluster is contacted.
- [LLM knowledge base](llm-kb-demo/) — raw notes on three fictional companies compile into linked wiki pages, with backlinks and an index. A question is answered from the pages it opens. A new note updates the pages it touches. From [Sebastian Krog’s post](https://x.com/SebKrog/status/2111837281568874577) and [his write-up](https://treasure-hunting.co/p/how-i-use-claude-as-my-second-brain) of [Andrej Karpathy’s LLM knowledge base](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2039805659525644595). No model is called.
- [Flowchart bench](pele-diagram-demo/) — a Mermaid subset (nodes, edges, labels, TD or LR) draws SVG on every keystroke and times that render. A slider builds a large graph. From [kepano’s launch of Pele](https://x.com/kepano/status/2111472054293913749). This page does not run Pele, and it does not report Pele’s or mermaid.js’s speed.
- [Benchmark traps](benchmark-traps-demo/) — a simulated database bench where a cold cache, a working set that fits in RAM, uneven cores, a short run, a closed loop, or a mean headline changes which engine wins. From [Ben Dicken’s post](https://x.com/BenjDicken/status/2112251037587583410) and [PlanetScale’s On benchmarking](https://planetscale.com/blog/on-benchmarking). No database is contacted.
- [Canvas chat](canvas-chat-demo/) — a scripted chat beside a canvas. Each reply is a plugin call that draws a map, a picture, a game, or a sortable ledger, and earlier canvases stay in the history. From [Dan Kornas’s post](https://x.com/DanKornas/status/2112419545889579016) about [MulmoChat](https://github.com/receptron/MulmoChat). No model is called.
- [Frame sound](earshot-demo/) — sixteen sounds made from oscillators, noise, envelopes, and filters, a kit derived from one character and a seed, and a looping title card whose hits are scheduled on exact frames. From [LexnLin’s post](https://x.com/LexnLin/status/2111927727318245426) about [earshot](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot) 0.3 and [cinetic](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/cinetic). The page does not run earshot. No model is called.
- [Verify loop](verify-loop-demo/) — a toy ledger and its replica pass their happy-path tests, then a seeded simulation reorders messages, drops an ack, crashes between a debit and a credit, and skews a clock. The same seed replays the failure. A scripted fix is applied and the budget runs again until it is clean. From [Geoffrey Huntley’s post](https://x.com/GeoffreyHuntley/status/2111432817980674225) about [overalls.dev](https://overalls.dev) and [Antithesis](https://antithesis.com). No model is called. The ledger is not Overalls.
- [Show me](show-me-demo/) — a scripted diff for a fictional notes inbox. The overview is pseudo-code rendered from the change, and `/show-me the InboxView` or `/show-me the preview cache` opens that part. Tokens are counted against the raw diff, and the slip copies as a PR description. From [Ben Holmes’s post](https://x.com/BHolmesDev/status/2111883582373257218) about [Dex Horthy’s /show-me](https://x.com/dexhorthy/status/2087569590268391897). No model is called.
- [Component canvas](component-canvas-demo/) — pan and zoom a board of live components that share one token set. Spawn a density, radius, or emphasis row beside one of them and promote a variation so every component follows. From [Ibelick’s post](https://x.com/Ibelick/status/2112530999447806236) on the [create-canvas skill](https://github.com/ibelick/ui-skills). No model is called.
- [Hackathon plan](hackathon-plan-demo/) — pick or type an idea, drag features onto one person’s five-week board, and read a judge card. The rules are the ones in [How to Win a Colosseum Hackathon](https://blog.colosseum.org/how-to-win-a-colosseum-hackathon/), via [Colosseum’s post](https://x.com/colosseum/status/1759965379957330040). Day counts are this page’s picture of that advice. Nothing is submitted.
- [Seed leak](seed-leak-demo/) — a toy wallet writes a session log. Toggle redaction, leaving the phrase out, encryption at rest, and who can read the store. Fictional phrases either sit on the server, reach the attacker, or stay put. From [OtterSec’s thread](https://x.com/osec_io/status/1555087555351420928) on the August 2022 Slope incident. Nothing onchain is contacted.
- [Code search](code-search-demo/) — a fictional twelve-file repo. Opening whole files is counted against ranked passages with file and line. BM25, a hand-built vector, and a hybrid can be switched, and symbol chunks can be turned off. From [Dan Kornas’s post](https://x.com/DanKornas/status/2113894860998525255) about [XERJ](https://github.com/xerj-org/xerj). No model is called. The page does not run XERJ.
- [Tests and proofs](formal-proof-demo/) — three toy functions pass a few tests. A bounded exhaustive check walks every 8-bit input, paints the ones that fail, and names a counterexample. From [Anthropic’s Frontier Red Team](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification), via [the post](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2113390212911083894), on work with [Theorem](https://theorem.dev/). This page is not Rocq, and the functions are not those libraries.
- [Dither bench](dither-bench-demo/) — a lit sphere, a gradient, a swirl, and a shaded globe, drawn with a threshold, Bayer 2x2, 4x4, or 8x8, white noise, or Floyd–Steinberg. Cell size and a two-colour pair change the plate. Hover a cell for the brightness and the threshold. From [Teknium’s post](https://x.com/Teknium/status/2113265212484636929) and [Uncommon web](https://webdev.teknium.io/). This page does not run dithercn or Paper Shaders.
- [Open factory](humanlayer-demo/) — a scripted task on a fictional refresh function. RPI Guided walks Questions, Research, Design, Structure, Plan, and Implement, and an open design comment holds the task. The scripted comment rewrites the design, and the diff follows. Oneshot implements directly and keeps the bare fetch. From [Dex Horthy’s post](https://x.com/dexhorthy/status/2113750268043210767) and [Open sourcing HumanLayer](https://www.humanlayer.dev/blog/open-sourcing-humanlayer). No model is called. This page does not run HumanLayer.
- [Scrollback compress](scrollback-compress-demo/) — a fake terminal streams a build log, a listing, JSON, a progress bar, or random noise into fixed-size pages. Pages off screen are deflated in the browser and inflated when you scroll back. The meters are this page’s bytes, not Ghostty’s. From [Mitchell Hashimoto’s Ghostty 1.4 release post](https://x.com/mitchellh/status/2115124583170974193).
- [Variation lab](variation-lab-demo/) — twenty variations of a fictional pricing card, split across layout, type, color, copy, and radius. Mix them, pin a few, and read a simulated click-through. Below, four theories about a slow list, each checked with a timer. From [Thorsten Ball’s “How I work with Agents.”](https://x.com/thorstenball/status/2114386050638336082).
- [Calendar design](calendar-design-demo/) — week, day, and month. Drag an empty hour to create, drag a block to move it, and pull the bottom edge to resize. A time-off request counts working days and skips weekends and holidays. Seeded on the week of 8–14 November 2026. From [@marcelkargul’s post](https://x.com/marcelkargul/status/2114866114136310061) and [kargulstudio/calender-app-design](https://github.com/kargulstudio/calender-app-design) (MIT). This page does not run that app.
- [Tiny cozy worlds](cozy-worlds-demo/) — a procedural island from a seed: terrain, water, trees, and a little house. Drag to turn it, slide from day to night, and reroll. The seed is the URL hash. From [@sucodeee’s post](https://x.com/sucodeee/status/2114658306526736491) and [MetalForge](https://metalforge.xyz). This page does not run MetalForge.
- [React internals](react-internals-demo/) — a toy scheduler and reconciler. Step the work loop, watch a click throw away a transition, and see one tree commit through a DOM view, an ASCII page, and a JSON log. From [Alexandre Trotel’s post](https://x.com/alexandretrotel/status/2115833209888076268) and [Understanding React internals](https://www.alexandretrotel.org/articles/understanding-react-internals). This page does not load React.
- [Portfolio roguelike](portfolio-roguelike-demo/) — a fictional studio page. Esc turns it into a small roguelike whose rooms are the projects. Arrow keys move, and a local board keeps the best runs. From [@michaelmicasso’s post](https://x.com/michaelmicasso/status/2115436121656332738) about [mektxt.com](https://mektxt.com). This page does not use that site’s art or code.
- [Statechart](statechart-demo/) — an espresso machine run by a hand-written statechart: nested states, parallel regions, a history latch, and guarded brew. The diagram lights the active states, and a switch counts the equivalent flat machine. From [David Khourshid’s article](https://x.com/i/article/2116505997439053824). XState is not loaded.
- [UpSet plot](upset-plot-demo/) — forty-two invented projects and six skills. Sort and filter the intersections, read the members, and set a three-skill Venn beside the same rows at five and six skills. From [ComplexUpset](https://github.com/krassowski/complex-upset). The rows are example data. R is not running.
- [Stage](stageshow-demo/) — a faux berth desk that starts as the mock an agent reaches for. Simplification, a recognizable screen, a crop, weight, a quiet look, and a caused motion each switch on their own. From [Benji Taylor’s post](https://x.com/benjitaylor/status/2116593247854453244) and the [stageshow skill](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills/blob/main/stageshow/SKILL.md), via [Shann Holmberg’s breakdown](https://x.com/shannholmberg/status/2116957574008590445). The page does not run the skill.
- [Message threads](message-threads-demo/) — the same five turns, twice. One lane resends the conversation. The other sends the new message and the previous response id, in the shape from [Anthropic’s message threads](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/message-threads). Bytes and the time-to-first-token bar are simulated. From [the Claude Developers post](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2117332449382510884). No API is called.
- [Sketch kit](sketch-kit-demo/) — a field card of buttons, a toggle, a checkbox, a slider, an input, tabs, and a progress bar. Outlines are pencil strokes from a seed. Roughness, stroke width, and the seed redraw them. The strokes are `createPencilPath` and `createPencilPoints` from [@inkra/react](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@inkra/react) 0.15.0, loaded from jsDelivr, with the same functions vendored if that module does not answer. From [YiJie’s post](https://x.com/yijie4188y/status/2116152311341092925) and [Inkra](https://inkrajs.github.io/). The React components are not loaded.
- [NEAR intents](near-intents-demo/) — a simulated swap or deposit. Solvers quote, the best `minAmountOut` that clears the floor wins, and settlement walks Solana, the NEAR Intents verifier, and the destination. If none qualifies, the intent expires and the wallet is unchanged. From [Aurora’s post](https://x.com/auroraisnear/status/2117296226571866245) and the [Aurora Intents docs](https://docs.intents.aurora.dev/). Prices are made up. No wallet is connected and no request is sent.
- [Interaction lab](interaction-lab-demo/) — six instruments: a damped spring, a cubic-bezier with 60 Hz ticks, a loupe, mercury you can fling, a torch cone, and a beaker whose surface stays level. From [Emil Kowalski’s post](https://x.com/emilkowalski/status/2117651655680463347) and [aiforui.dev/lab](https://aiforui.dev/lab). The instruments are written in this folder.
- [Meme mechanics](meme-mechanics-demo/) — a fictional token on a constant-product curve, a per-wallet cap, a staking pool that shares the trade fee, a treasury buyback-and-burn, and a lock-then-mint bridge. Bots share one clock. From [@memedotcooking](https://x.com/memedotcooking/status/2117206516927168704) and [NEAR Builders](https://x.com/NearBuilders/status/2117665200015057228) on the Memecoins With Mechanics track with Shitzu. No wallet is connected and no request is sent.

## Open locally

From this directory:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Open [http://127.0.0.1:4173](http://127.0.0.1:4173).
