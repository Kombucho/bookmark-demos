Travel is on. Digits roll by place, and shared letters slide.

A digit that changes rolls through the steps between, in the direction the value moved. A comma is its own place: it steps in when the figure grows, and the decimal point stays put. A character both lines share keeps its identity and slides. The rest leave upward or arrive from below. The whole string does not fade as one piece.

The continuity is the idea in Torph, from @GabiDev98’s bookmark of lochieaxon’s text and number morphs. This page does not install that package. The reels and the match are written here. If the system asks for reduced motion, Snap starts on.
