Frame sound

A library you can audition, a kit derived from one character and a seed, and a looping title card. Each hit is scheduled on a frame. Change the frame rate and the frame number stays; the clock time moves.

No model is called. earshot is not running. Every tone on this page is an oscillator, noise, an envelope, and a filter. Sound stays off until you press a button.

Library

Sixteen named sounds. Press one to hear it and draw its envelope.

Kit

One character, one seed. Click, pop, whoosh, tick, hit, success, and error share a root.

    earshot 0.3 ships a bank of 201 sounds stored as code, rendered by id with no models and no network. earshot sfx aims a sound at a frame.
