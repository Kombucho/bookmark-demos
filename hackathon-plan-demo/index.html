Judge card

    What a 3-minute pitch could say

      The board is one person’s weekdays across five weeks. Weeks 1–4 are the engineering sprint. Week 5 is testing and the pitch. A technical cofounder adds a second build track. A non-technical cofounder takes the project account and the beta session off your build track. Empty days are unused time. “At least four weeks” is why those four rows are there. It is not a rule that every cell has to be filled. Work from before the hackathon is not an extra row: the article says projects are judged on what was done during the hackathon, even if a team started two months earlier.

      Colosseum’s post says the next Solana hackathon kicked off March 4, 2024, on Colosseum, and that the article covers team formation, product ideation, prioritization and execution, and presentation. March 4 is that announcement. It is not a slot on this calendar. The article’s own structure is an online hackathon, five weeks long, no specific bounties, and Solana integration as the remaining tech requirement. Prizes, it says, go to teams who intend to build full-time and who have a possibly viable business, and there is always an award for the best public good. It also says there are usually 40 individual prizes and that the top 100 are typically exceptional. This card does not place you among them.

      Hivemapper and Tensor are the article’s examples of a market brought onchain and of a product built for a pain the founders had. Kiln, Streetlamp, and Wick are not those products. The cofounder switch does not introduce you to anyone. The article says to look at your own network first, then the Cofounder Directory.

      From Colosseum’s post and How to Win a Colosseum Hackathon. Nothing on this page is sent anywhere.
