          Source
          
    
          From minichain.tech, @Rhynotic’s “minichain: an entire blockchain in an ethereum smart contract.” A whole chain lives in one Ethereum contract. Users deposit ETH, deploy guest contracts, call them, transfer, and withdraw. Guest code and storage sit in the chain’s state and run in an interpreter. MiniVM has 16 registers and four-byte instructions. An EVM interpreter is the other built-in engine. Execution is bounded by a step budget on the call, separate from gas. ETH is the only asset, and every internal wei is backed by ETH the contract holds. A fee is a transfer inside that same ledger, so “held by the contract” stays equal to “owed inside.” Each operation commits one block: the head becomes the hash of the previous head, the height, and the operation, and the height goes up by one. There is no token, no sequencer, and no bridge.
          
    
          The counter is the 20-byte program from the docs: 0x130100001602000003020201170002001a020000. It loads the first input word, adds the slot, stores it, and returns. This page runs that bytecode. The fee here is a flat 0.001 ETH on deploy, call, and transfer, so the internal transfer is easy to see. On the real chain the fee is about a tenth of the host gas, split between the chain’s owner and the owner of MiniChain #1, and deposits and withdrawals pay none. The hash is SHA-256 from crypto.subtle, not the chain’s hash. The guest address is a label for this page, not a CREATE address. Nothing is sent to Ethereum.
