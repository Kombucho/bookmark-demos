The beaker is marked to 240 ml. Spilled liquid does not climb back in. P₀ is (0, 0) and P₃ is (1, 1); the sixty ticks are one second at 60 Hz. If the system asks for reduced motion, the spring does not travel, the bead stays put, and the metal does not coast. The readouts still follow the pointer.

In the spirit of Emil Kowalski’s Interaction Lab (aiforui.dev/lab). These six instruments are written in this folder. They do not use that page’s code or assets.
