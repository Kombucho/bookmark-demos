Canvas

Each card is a micro-branch. Select one to put it on the phone. Fork from any card, including one you have left behind.

The agent is a short list of recipes in this folder. It does not call a model. Rejected branches stay on the canvas with their reason, and a later fork skips a recipe that would rebuild that exact screen.

The canvas is stored in this browser under bookmark-demos.micro-branch.v1.

From Vincent Nallatamby’s thread on Quest’s AI iOS designer workflow. The designer prompts against the real screen. Iterations are micro-branches on a review canvas, so the designer and the product lead can move back and forth without the next pass erasing the last. A fine detail, such as a button’s corner radius, is tuned on a temporary panel in the prototype, then the panel is removed. A reply on the thread: put a one-line rejection on a branch so later exploration does not rebuild it.
