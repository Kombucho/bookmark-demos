Straight lines

A portrait, a range, and a spiral. The tone is the gap between strokes. Turn the angle, open the spacing, change the weight. The subject is redrawn from the same straight marks.

Subject

Angle turns the hatch. Spacing opens the tone. Weight is the stroke.

Each mark runs from one point to another. A darker passage is that same stroke, set closer, and past a point a second pass crosses it. Nothing here is a curve, and the shade is not a filled shape. The outline is segments too.

Block in lays a polyline on the sheet. On release the last point meets the first, and the hatch fills that region with the angle and spacing already on the bench. Reroll nudges the construction and shifts where the strokes start. The subject stays the one you picked.

Straight strokes as the whole of the drawing, the constraint in Sunny Art’s line drawings, from @pmamtraveller’s post. The three plates are built in this folder.
