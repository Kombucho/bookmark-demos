Door rules check

    From @BetweenYears26’s post on Between Years, a 32-door calendar meant to live in a wallet pass. Community pieces would sit behind the doors. The rules named there: no wallet connection and no signatures, files only, no server, no outside calls, no trackers, fast on a phone, and saves kept in the browser. This page is an unofficial mock of that pass. It does not create a pass.
