This browser asked for less motion, so the shot stays on the poster even when Motion is on.

Start from AI default, then turn the rules on one at a time. Crop should push the window off the bottom of the hero tile. Weight should make that tile the large one and pull the slip halfway off the window’s edge. Motion plays the assign. The poster, with Motion off, is the frame after it lands.

From Benji Taylor’s post announcing Stageshow (npx skills add benjitaylor/skills --skill stageshow) and Shann Holmberg’s breakdown. The switches quote stageshow/SKILL.md: “It reads as a real product, simplified to what the viewer needs, framed with intent, and every motion has a cause.” Crop, the straddling slip, the word budget, the ban on sparkles and invented marks, and the poster are the rules in references/framing.md, references/craft.md, and references/motion.md. The grid beside the window is the bento idea in references/bento.md: one hero, and the other tiles are a number and a sentence, not more windows. Keel is fictional. This page does not run the skill, React, or the film scripts. Nothing is fetched.
