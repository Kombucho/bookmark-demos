Knowledge factory

Context is what an agent has for this run. Knowledge is the card that should still be right tomorrow. Three agents work the same jobs each morning. They can flag a stale card. They do not edit it. At night a curator reads the flags, the traces, and the raw notes, and rewrites the store.

Fictional example. Northline Analytics, Mara Chen, Ivo Park, the agents, the warehouse names, and every figure are made up. No model is called.
