          The four rules this page actually uses
          1. Out of scope. A sentence with won’t, don’t, not a, not the, later, never, skip, or “can wait” is a no. Hedge words get stripped so “we are not building a full CRM” becomes “A full CRM.”
          2. Success. A sentence with a percent, or words like success, goal, fewer, faster, or under, is the finish line.
          3. Users. A role word (team, customer, manager, designer, parent, teacher, kid, and similar) is plucked out, plus up to three words in front. Small words such as the, our, for, and because stop the grab. A role word that only describes the next word (“parent social network”) is skipped.
          4. Problem. Whatever is left, minus fluff like basically, honestly, just, and really, is the problem.

          One sentence lands in one pile. Users are pulled from every sentence. Nothing is sent anywhere.
