November 2026

Week of 8–14 November 2026. Drag on the grid to add an event.

Keys: T today, arrows to move the period, D W M for the view, N new event, R request time off. With an event focused, arrows nudge it and Backspace deletes it.

Rebuilt in the spirit of Kargul Studio’s calendar design (MIT). The week of 8–14 November 2026 is the one that prototype opens on. This page does not run that app, and it does not use its assets. From @marcelkargul’s post.
