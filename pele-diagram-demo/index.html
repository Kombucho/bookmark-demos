Flowchart on a keystroke

Type a small Mermaid flowchart. Nodes, edge labels, and TD or LR are drawn to SVG on every keystroke, and the clock is the time that keystroke spent drawing.

This renderer is a subset written for the page. It is not Pele, and the time is not a Pele or mermaid.js result. Pele’s published comparison is not measured or repeated here.

    The number includes parsing the text, laying out the ranks, building the SVG, and inserting it. A frame at 60Hz is 16.7ms. A fast machine hides a small graph. The slider grows the same drawing until the time is large enough to feel on a keystroke.
