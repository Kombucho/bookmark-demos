Ceramic instruments

Four faces, written as SVG paths and extruded into clearcoat ceramic. Their outlines draw themselves in. Drag the bar: glaze on one side, and a wireframe, normals, or flat clay on the other.

Glaze

Wireframe

The outlines are drawing.

Drag the empty glaze to orbit. Drag the bar, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to wipe. On a touch screen, drag to turn and drag the bar to wipe.

  1. SVGDial, gauge, ruler, and knob are paths in this page. SVGLoader reads them. The drawings sit under the view.
  2. CeramicEach path is extruded. MeshPhysicalMaterial, clearcoat, and a soft room environment make the glaze.
  3. drawRangeThe outlines are buffer geometries. setDrawRange grows from nothing to the full line.
  4. ScissorThe canvas is drawn twice with setScissor. The right pass sets scene.overrideMaterial.
