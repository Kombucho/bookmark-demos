State a swap or a deposit. Simulated solvers send competing quotes. The best quote whose guarantee clears your minimum wins, then settlement walks from Solana through the NEAR Intents verifier to the destination. If none clears, the intent expires and the wallet is unchanged.

      Amounts in the JSON are illustrative 6-decimal atoms, including NEAR, so the card stays readable. Real NEAR amounts are yocto (10⁻²⁴). quoteWaitingTimeMs is 3000, the message-bus wait. Slippage is 100 bps, so minAmountOut is 99% of amountOut. EXPIRED here is the quote window closing before any deposit: nothing was transferred, so there is nothing to refund. A deposit that had already moved would be a different failure. Deposit with custom execution adds OPERATION_PENDING and OPERATION_PROCESSING, the destination call Intents Connect runs after the funds arrive. Northglass is not a real contract.
