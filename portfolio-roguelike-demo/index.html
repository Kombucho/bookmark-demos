Projects

01 · Catalog

Harbor Index

A catalog for a maritime library that never opened. Entries are a title, a year, and one sentence. The search sits in the margin and does not follow you down the page.

Type is a text face at a size you can read on a wet clipboard. The paper color is the library’s, not the screen’s.

02 · Identity

Kiln

An identity for the Greyhaven ceramics school. The wordmark is a bowl in profile. The palette is four slips: chalk, iron, celadon, and the brown of a used wheel.

The course list is one page. No photographs of hands. The school’s name is set so it still reads in clay dust.

03 · Product

Low Lamp

A notes app for a night editor. One column, no avatars, no presence dots. A note is a title and a body, and the export is a text file with the line breaks left alone.

The lamp in the corner is the only chrome. It dims the page. It does not notify anyone.

04 · Wayfinding

Sunday Route

Maps for the Merrow walking club. Each sheet is a single afternoon. The type is large enough to read with the paper folded once, held at a stile, in a coat pocket’s shade.

Turns are sentences, not icons. “Leave the oak on your left” survives a smudge better than a chevron does.

About

Nera Voss designs interfaces and the type that has to live in them. Fieldnote is the studio name on the invoice. Before it, a stretch of book covers and one civic form that had to be filled in on a phone, in the rain, without an account.

The work on this page is fictional. Greyhaven, Merrow, and the library are not places you can visit. The floor you can walk — press Esc — is the same four projects, plus the reading room and the letter office, laid out as rooms.
